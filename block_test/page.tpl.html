Parental block test of the hybrid assemblies

Short reads of two parental-species animals were mapped to each assembly of a hybrid (minimap2, short-read mode; reads with mapping quality ≥ 30 counted): for D. unisexualis, D. valentini 5_38 and D. raddei nairensis 17_78; for D. armeniaca, two pairs of animals not used to split its assembly (D. valentini 38_75 with D. mixta DM2, and 33_75 with DM3). In each 100-kb block the differences per aligned base were summed for each animal. The block is called for the animal whose reads differ less, when the margin is at least 25% and each animal has at least 300 reads in it.

2. One unn contig, block by block

Section 1 counts blocks; this section follows them along one contig. A run of blocks called for the wrong parent, inside a contig that otherwise matches the expected parent, is a switch: the assembly joined a piece of the other subgenome to this contig. Such pieces are rare in all four assemblies, and only one of them carries a CLsat array:
