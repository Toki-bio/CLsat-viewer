# *Darevskia armeniaca* ortholog plates (revision panel, run_z3, night 8 November 2026)

Alignment plates of CLsat arrays in the father (armf) and mother (armm) copies of *D. armeniaca* 3_04 against the
*D. valentini* 218 and *D. mixta* 80 Drafts. Flanks are lower case, catalogue arrays (run_z3 segments) upper case;
arrays longer than 1,200 letters are cut to their first and last 500 letters joined by 20 N. Windows are assembly
coordinates (0-based start); the label after the window gives the array position inside the window.
Aligned with mafft --auto --preservecase --adjustdirection; `reverse_complement` marks a turned sequence.
Analysis: `C:/work/CLsat/review/night_2026-11-08/T4_VARIABILITY.md`, `T4_pairs.tsv`.
